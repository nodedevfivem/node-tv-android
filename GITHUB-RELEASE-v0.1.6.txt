# 📺 NODE TV Android v0.1.6 — Floating Channel Browser

NODE Development | Android

Introducing a cleaner way to find and change channels while your current television program stays on screen.

## 📺 NEW: FLOATING BROWSE CHANNELS PANEL

- Replaced the cramped two-row portrait channel list with a dedicated **BROWSE CHANNELS** button.
- Open a dark translucent bottom sheet with the active TV playback visible behind it.
- See more compact channel rows at once, with smooth independent scrolling for long directories.
- Search by channel title or category directly within the floating browser.
- Filter Worldwide, Philippines, Favorites and Recently Watched.
- Choose an individual channel category from the category dropdown.
- Highlight the currently playing channel with a **▶ LIVE** badge and theme accent.
- Favorite/unfavorite channels directly from the browse list.
- Select a channel to switch streams and automatically close the browser.
- Close without interrupting playback using **✕ CLOSE**, Android Back, outside tap or swipe down from the sheet handle.
- Removed long channel lists and filter controls from the main portrait player screen for a cleaner layout.
- Reused efficient ListView row recycling and existing channel repository; no backend or hosting required.

## 📡 STREAMING AND CONTROLS PRESERVED

- Media3 ExoPlayer primary playback and VideoLAN LibVLC fallback.
- v0.1.5 stable video surface approach retained; no video surface reparenting while browsing.
- Retro wooden TV, original NODE TV branding and eight saved themes.
- Landscape fullscreen, on-screen EXIT FULLSCREEN, brightness and volume gestures, center-tap pause/play.
- CRT static/no-reception recovery, channel search, favorites and recently watched.
- GitHub automatic update banner, persistent mandatory update handling and installer checksums.
